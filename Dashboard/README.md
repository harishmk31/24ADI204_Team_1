# 🏏 IPL Player Analysis Dashboard  
### 📊 Data Science & Visualization Project (24ADI204)

---

## 🚀 Project Title  
**Data-Driven IPL Insights: A Moneyball Approach to Player Valuation**

---

## 🎯 Objective  
This project analyzes the IPL 2025 auction dataset to understand how player attributes such as **age, role, experience, and nationality** influence player valuation.  
The goal is to identify **hidden patterns** and **undervalued players** using a data-driven approach.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview  

The **IPL Player Analysis Dashboard** provides an interactive and visual representation of player pricing trends, role distribution, and performance factors.

### 🔹 Key Metrics (Top KPIs)
- **Total Players:** 575  
- **Average Price:** 61 Lakhs  
- **Maximum Price:** 200 Lakhs  
- **Average Age:** 27  

---

## 📈 Visualizations Included  

### 📌 1. Reserve Price by Player  
- Displays top players based on total reserve price  
- Helps identify high-value players  

### 📌 2. Average Price by Role  
- Compares pricing across roles  
- Shows that **Bowlers and All-rounders dominate pricing**

### 📌 3. Role Distribution (Donut Chart)  
- Shows proportion of players by role  
- Highlights majority role categories  

### 📌 4. Price Trend by Experience  
- Shows how experience impacts pricing  
- Insight: **Highly experienced players have higher value**

### 📌 5. Age vs Player Price (Scatter Plot)  
- Visualizes relationship between age and pricing  
- Insight: Peak pricing occurs around **25–30 age group**

### 📌 6. Players by Country  
- Shows player distribution across countries  
- Insight: **Indian players dominate the auction pool**

---

## 🧠 Key Insights  

- Experience is the **strongest factor** influencing price  
- Capped players are valued significantly higher  
- All-rounders provide higher overall value  
- Price distribution is **right-skewed** due to elite players  
- Overseas players tend to have premium pricing  
- Market inefficiencies allow identification of undervalued players
